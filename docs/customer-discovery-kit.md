# Club Watch customer-discovery kit

This implements the repository-owned preparation in `D2.6–D2.9`. Ryan owns recruitment,
interviews, recording consent, and paid-pilot invitations.

## Segment screener

Ask without naming Club Watch:

1. Which club do you follow most closely?
2. In the last four matchweeks, how often did you check what a result meant for its season target?
3. Which target mattered: title, promotion, playoff, qualification, relegation, or something else?
4. What did you use—standings, score app, forum, local reporter, model, spreadsheet, or another
   source?
5. Describe the most recent time you combined more than one source to understand the race.
6. Have you paid for club news, analytics, a supporter membership, fantasy tools, or sports
   subscriptions in the last year?
7. Is your club in an active consequential race now?

Primary-fan qualification requires a specific club, a recent real monitoring episode, an active
race, and evidence of repeated effort. Quantitative users and creators are tagged separately and
never averaged into the primary persona.

## Recent-behavior interview guide

1. “Take me back to the last matchweek when a result changed how you saw the season.”
2. “What did you do first after the final whistle?”
3. “What did you check next, and why was the first source not enough?”
4. “How long did it take before you felt you understood the consequence?”
5. “What answer were you trying to get?”
6. “What was confusing, missing, late, or untrustworthy?”
7. “When did you next check the race?”
8. “What happens when the club is safe, eliminated, clinched, or out of season?”
9. “What have you paid for that helps with this job? What made it worth paying?”
10. “If your current sources disappeared tomorrow, what would you replace them with?”

Do not demo features until the behavior account is complete. Ask separately for recording,
follow-up, paid-pilot contact, and attributable-quote consent.

## Coding frame

Code each note with:

- recurring job and desired outcome;
- trigger and urgency;
- current alternatives and sequence;
- time/effort/frustration;
- trust requirement;
- payment evidence;
- objection;
- offseason/clinched/eliminated behavior;
- paid-pilot response;
- disconfirming evidence.

The synthesis reports primary fans, quant fans, and creators separately. Praise, hypothetical
interest, and feature requests are not payment evidence.
