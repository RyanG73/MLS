# Full-price Club Watch concierge kit

**Status:** Prepared; do not recruit or charge until the `D2`, commercial, measurement, and trust
gates pass.

## Disclosure and onboarding

Use plain language: this is a four-to-six-week, manually reviewed concierge pilot. The customer is
buying the Club Watch outcome, not automated email. Record club, target, timezone, acquisition
source, preferred cadence, notification ceiling, and consent choices.

## Candidate update

1. **What changed:** before/after probability, target, timestamp, and receipt.
2. **Why:** own result, rival result, schedule change, or new model evidence. Use “supported by,”
   not invented certainty.
3. **What next:** next relevant fixture and W/D/L conditional stakes.
4. **Relevant rival:** include only when it materially explains the race.
5. **Confidence/limitation:** data state, freshness, thin history, or correction.
6. **Reviewer verdict:** send, suppress as trivial, correct, or escalate.

## Review checklist

- Correct club, competition, target, season, and next fixture.
- Probability and movement match the current source snapshot.
- No bootstrap/reset artifact is presented as movement.
- Cause links to evidence.
- W/D/L labels use the followed club’s point of view.
- Low-data limitations are visible.
- No duplicate, cap breach, unsupported certainty, or unapproved email claim.

## Delivery and feedback ledger

For every candidate record:

`candidate_id`, club, match cycle, generated time, reviewed time, reviewer decision, delivered
time, channel, viewed, clicked, ignored, corrected, support triggered, reason suppressed, and
evidence IDs.

After two cycles ask:

- Which update saved time or changed understanding?
- Which felt obvious, trivial, late, or noisy?
- What would the customer have used if Entenser disappeared?
- Was free lookup sufficient?
- Would they make a binding second-month choice at the ordinary price?

Weekly evidence separates observed counts from interpretation and reports non-buyers as well as
buyers.
