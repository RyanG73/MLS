## Global language rules

- Customer name: **Entenser Club Watch**. Internal API/entitlement alias: `intel`.
- “Live” must be qualified as full forecast, preseason, fixtures limited, results only, archive,
  stale, or unavailable.
- Predictions can be wrong. Public methods, calibration, model health, receipts, and misses remain
  free.
- Entenser is independent and unaffiliated with a club unless a real agreement says otherwise.
- Do not use betting-pick, profit, staking, or affiliate positioning.
