## Metric contract

- **Active paid:** a subscriber with a current paid entitlement who is not refunded, expired, or
  in an unrecovered failed-payment state. Scheduled cancellation remains active until expiration.
- **Engaged paid:** an active paid subscriber who consumed at least one core Club Watch value event
  in the trailing 30 days.
- **Activation:** the user follows a club and consumes one complete personalized Club Watch update.
- **Core value event:** a material-change explanation, next-match stakes view, or since-last-visit
  update consumed for a followed club.

## Commercialization rule

Opening checkout requires all commercial, measurement, trust, and claim-truth gates in the active
plan. Transaction readiness alone does not authorize promotion. Live email remains a separate
decision after two complete shadow matchweeks, one quiet-mode cycle, and explicit owner approval.
